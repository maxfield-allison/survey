# Contributing

Start with a concrete request, the observed result and the intended result. Preserve useful decision methods and scope changes deliberately. Keep the entrypoint focused on the task; place conditional detail in relative references.

Check discovery metadata and resource closure separately from behavior. For behavior changes, compare the baseline and candidate under matched cases, tools, model and harness. Retain failed and interrupted trials as well as successful ones. A model's statement that it loaded a skill is insufficient evidence of loading.

Predeclare the acceptance criterion and stopping rule. Use multiple trials for variable outcomes and new held-out cases before claiming generalization. Prepared cases and static checks are not trial results. Avoid installing tools or launching metered evaluations as incidental fallbacks.

Keep private decision records and evaluator expectations outside the installed package. Review third-party provenance and rights before including external material. Contributions are licensed under Apache-2.0.
