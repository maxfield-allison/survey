# Survey

Compare credible options and recommend a course from evidence. Survey starts with the decision and its constraints, maps the alternatives, investigates what could change the choice, and tests the strongest counter-case before giving a judgement.

It's useful when you need a wider search before choosing or a fresh look at an existing approach. You can work with local records and tests, external research, or both. The method scales to the question and keeps decisive unknowns visible.

## Use the skill

Keep [the complete skill directory](skills/survey/SKILL.md) together and load it through your harness's existing skill workflow. Its references cover [evidence practice](skills/survey/references/evidence.md) and [durable reports](skills/survey/references/report.md).

Example request:

> Use Survey to compare retaining our current approach with the credible alternatives. Judge them against recovery and upkeep, test the strongest counter-case, and recommend the next step with evidence and remaining unknowns.

You'll get a recommendation and its conditions. Sustained external research can support it. You don't need the full method for a narrow code review or a factual lookup. Use the ordinary workflow for that task.

It's a directory of instructions and references, with no executable helper. You don't need a particular provider or tracker layout, and it doesn't require agent fan-out. Check that your harness loads it and routes the right requests to it. Then try representative tasks. Static package checks won't establish task quality.
