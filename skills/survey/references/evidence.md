# Evidence for a decision

Scale the record to the decision. A short recommendation can cite its few sources inline;
an extended or contested investigation needs a reusable register. Do not turn every survey
into a systematic review. That claim requires a predefined reviewed search and screening
protocol, with the appropriate literature-review workflow.

For material claims, record a stable claim ID, statement, status (measured, reported, inferred
or unknown), supporting source IDs, contrary evidence and the limit on the conclusion. Keep
assumptions distinct from observations. Mark an inference with its reasoning rather than
giving it the status of its strongest citation.

For each used source, retain its ID, title, author or organization, URL or repository path,
retrieval date, publication/update date if available, revision or relevant locator, source
type and underlying origin. Record unknown dates and revisions as unknown. Preserve a
permitted excerpt or observation that actually supports the claim; retaining an entire
private document is usually unnecessary.

Evaluate sources against the specific question: direct observation versus reported result,
methods and conditions, incentive or conflict, recency, applicable geography/platform/version,
and independence. A primary source can still be weak or interested. A preprint is not
peer-reviewed merely because it is a research paper. Prefer the strongest relevant evidence,
not an automatic source hierarchy that discards contrary observations.

For extended searches, retain the question/subquestion, tool or database, exact query,
filters, date and useful result or access failure. Use terminology from multiple plausible
explanations and seek negative evidence. Check whether ranking, language, region or access
limits shaped the results. Follow promising secondary claims back to their origin. Record
material exclusions and why they were excluded, without cataloguing every irrelevant hit.

Before delivery, check that each decisive claim has supporting evidence or an explicit
uncertainty; citations support their attached claims; repeated sources have not been counted
as independent; and contradictions survive into the judgement. Confidence follows evidence
fitness and remaining uncertainty, not a minimum number of links.

Stop at the declared limit or when unresolved questions cannot change the recommendation.
State which occurred. If an unresolved question could change it, give the smallest resolving
test, responsible decision and conditional outcome. Search exhaustion is not proof of absence.
